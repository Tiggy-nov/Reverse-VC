# Sources and acknowledgments

Reverse VC is an original synthesis of analytical instructions, reusable review methods and templates. Sources inform questions; they do not validate a particular company or endorse this project.

- Chris Tottman, [I asked Claude to reject my pitch until it couldn't. Then I raised.](https://www.the-founders-corner.com/p/i-asked-claude-to-reject-my-pitch). Informed cumulative questioning, a strong investment-committee bear case, evidence-based responses and learning from investor meetings. The source PDF is excluded from this repository. This suite uses its own instructions, adds product/technology and operating-capacity analysis, and retains uncertainty, bounded iteration and neutral final review.
- Bessemer Venture Partners, [The State of AI 2025](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/the-state-of-ai-2025). Informed questions about AI growth, retention and economics; selected company examples are not universal benchmarks.
- Andreessen Horowitz, [The New Business of AI](https://a16z.com/the-new-business-of-ai-and-how-its-different-from-traditional-software/). Informed attention to compute, human delivery and deployment costs; historical numeric ranges are not adopted as current hurdles.
- Anthropic, [Demystifying evals for AI agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents). Informed explicit tasks, criteria and evaluation limitations.
- Andreessen Horowitz, [Lighthouse or Landgrab? How to Pick Your AI Sales Strategy](https://a16z.com/lighthouse-or-landgrab-how-to-pick-your-ai-sales-strategy/). Informed buyer-proof and sales-motion questions, without adopting categorical prescriptions.
- Bessemer Venture Partners, [Design partners: The pre-launch edge most AI founders ignore](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/design-partners-the-pre-launch-edge-most-ai-founders-ignore). Informed distinctions between feedback, commitment, production and renewal.
- Sequoia Capital, [Retention](https://articles.sequoiacap.com/retention). Informed natural usage cadence and comparable cohorts.

Additional source links and their limitations are preserved in the relevant skills. Reopen sources and verify current facts for each real review. Repeated investor assertions and AI agreement are not independent commercial evidence.
