# VC Financial Model and Capital

Does the model faithfully represent the business, and does its cash and financing plan support the claimed milestones?

## Company and fundraising lens

Default to AI-native B2B and enterprise businesses at Seed or Series A. Read [company-calibration.md](references/company-calibration.md) first, or reuse an established source-backed context: product/workflow, market and buyer, business model, actual maturity and proposed current raise. Apply only relevant tests and follow an explicit user scope override.

When the fundraising amount, timing or use of proceeds changes this review, read [raise-calibration.md](references/raise-calibration.md). Connect capital to the specific proof and operating milestones it buys; distinguish requested primary capital, secondary, secured funds and existing cash. If the amount is missing, continue the domain work and request it only when needed for the capital conclusion. A larger raise does not itself establish maturity, and a narrow test does not require a full financing audit.

State the applied business/stage lens and any material effect of the raise on the result. Keep current evidence, capital-plan feasibility and investor fit distinct; share definitions and source IDs across reviews.

## Scope and intake

Use the native workbook and linked schedules where available, accounting/billing records, metric definitions, cash/debt obligations and proposed financing terms. For one metric or runway question, inspect the relevant schedules and dependencies first; a PDF-only review remains limited to visible figures.

Specify whether you inspected displayed values, formulas, source reconciliations or recalculated results. This is scoped diligence, not an assurance opinion. Commercial assumptions, product quality and market demand require their own evidence; a balanced spreadsheet does not validate them.

This is a standalone specialist: it does not require Reverse VC Scaling or another skill. The user can supply a full room and request one test; inspect the relevant evidence and keep the conclusion scoped to that test.

## Review

Read [review-method.md](references/review-method.md) for evidence handling, Seed/Series A calibration, test design, review limits and optional revision/retest behavior. Read the applicable tests in [domain-tests.md](references/domain-tests.md); choose by the decision at risk rather than running every question.

Default to an evidence-grounded review. For an explicit founder rehearsal, ask one question at a time and wait. For requested repair, produce supported local revisions and retest affected claims. No source materials means an intake or proposed test, not a verdict.

## Deliver

Return source-to-model reconciliations, material formula/definition defects, coherent scenarios, cash minima and financing deadlines, and term-dependent return calculations where requested. Separate cash survival, investment potential and document quality.

Include exact source locators and coverage limitations, the strongest supported point, material objections, and what would change the answer. Distinguish an inspected result from an unexecuted experiment. Preserve unresolved cross-domain dependencies without presenting this review as a full-company approval.

If a coordinating review exists, reuse its source/claim IDs, periods and metric definitions and return the compact handoff specified in the review method. If this skill is used alone, create only the evidence record needed for its task. No external model or additional specialist needs to be installed.
