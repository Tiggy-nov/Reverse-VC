# Team, execution and governance

Question: does the team have the capabilities, decision mechanisms and capacity to deliver the next stage? Use [the shared test contract](../challenge-loop.md). Evaluate relevant actions and documented responsibilities, not founder charisma, hesitation or demographic proxies.

Inputs where authorized: roles, track record relevant to the problem, org/capability plan, hiring and retention evidence, decision rights, operating milestones, founder/board arrangements and cap-table/vesting information where material.

## High-value tests

1. **Specific execution advantage.** Ask what this team has demonstrated or can access that changes its probability of solving this problem. Inspect relevant shipped products, customer access, domain learning and repeatable delivery. General pedigree is not a substitute for evidence; nor is lack of prestige evidence against capability.
2. **Current capability gap.** Map each next-stage milestone to an accountable role and demonstrated capacity. Identify the missing capability and the business risk while it remains missing. Distinguish a planned hire from a recruited leader; price recruitment/ramp and bridge responsibilities into the model. Propose the next hires only where supported by the plan, rather than inventing a fixed number.
3. **Decision and conflict mechanism.** Ask for a real consequential disagreement, who decided, how the decision was made, and the result. Inspect formal authority and escalation where provided. Equity or title differences are prompts to understand incentives, not proof of dysfunction. Separate management statements, documented terms and reviewer interpretations; do not infer conflict from missing private records.
4. **Key-person and scaling exposure.** Test the departure/unavailability of a pivotal technical, commercial or operational leader. Assess knowledge concentration, delegation, succession, recruiting and handoff capability. Tie the scenario to product delivery, selling, customer obligations and cash rather than treating every founder dependency as equally material.

Output: capability-to-milestone map, substantiated execution strengths, unresolved governance questions, and proposed mitigation/hiring tests with costs and owners where known. Do not make founder commitments or perform reference outreach without authorization.

Handoffs: hiring/payroll/lead times → finance; deployment and selling leadership → scalability; IP/technical continuity → product; customer access claims → market/competition. Keep sensitive personnel observations within the authorized internal review audience.
