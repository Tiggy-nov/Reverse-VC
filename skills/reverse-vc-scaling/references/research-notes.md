# Research starting points and freshness

These sources informed selected review questions. The workflow and templates are an original synthesis, not a claim that any named investor endorses them. Checked 2026-09-26. Reopen relevant sources and search for newer evidence when reviewing a real company; never treat these links as evidence about that company.

| Primary source | Relevance | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bessemer, The State of AI 2025](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/the-state-of-ai-2025), published 2025-08-13 | Distinguishes different growth and margin patterns among selected AI companies; motivates reviewing growth alongside retention and economics | A selected group of standout companies is not a representative universal hurdle. Preserve cohort, date, and metric definitions when using benchmarks |
| [a16z, The New Business of AI](https://a16z.com/the-new-business-of-ai-and-how-its-different-from-traditional-software/), published 2020-02-16 | Raises the economic significance of compute, ongoing human work, and deployment differences from conventional software | Historical conceptual source. Do not reuse its numeric ranges as present-day AI margins or assume old technical constraints still apply |
| [Anthropic, Demystifying evals for AI agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents), published 2026-01-09 | Distinguishes tasks, trials, graders, and observed outcomes; supports explicit criteria, repeated trials where useful, and regression checks | General evaluation engineering guidance, not evidence that a model can predict investment outcomes or substitute for commercial diligence |

For a current claim, prefer the source closest to the fact: customer/contract and accounting records for private commercial data; official product/pricing documentation for competitors and providers; statistical agencies or transparent underlying datasets for market counts; applicable official authorities for legal rules. A primary vendor or company claim may still be self-reported.

Record publication or reporting date separately from access date. Save definitions, scope, methods, sample limitations, geography and period with any benchmark. If the original cannot be inspected, mark verification incomplete. Search tools can help find evidence; their summaries do not establish it.

## Reference guide incorporated 2026-09-27

Chris Tottman, [I asked Claude to reject my pitch until it couldn't. Then I raised.](https://www.the-founders-corner.com/p/i-asked-claude-to-reject-my-pitch), dated 2026-05-11 in the reviewed edition. It is a practical playbook, not empirical validation of fundraising outcomes.

Adapted ideas: cumulative adversarial questioning (pp. 7–9), market and return logic (9–11), mechanism-based defensibility (14–17), cost/cohort scrutiny (17–20), team/governance (20–22), timing counterfactuals (23–25), buyer alternatives (25–27), the IC bear case (27–29), and a meeting preparation/debrief learning loop (29–34). Its separation of underlying business problems from presentation problems (34–35) remains central.

The modules and prompts here are original adaptations. Product/technology evaluation, operational scale, full model mechanics, and a distinct category-creation test extend beyond the guide's six core challenge categories. We retain evidence qualification, bounded iteration and a fresh neutral final review. We do not adopt blanket numerical thresholds, a required exact month for every timing argument, uncaveated objections, or mind-reading from meeting demeanor. Source statistics and anecdotes require separate verification before use as investment evidence.
