# Primary-source context

Reviewed 2026-09-27. These sources inform selected questions; this skill's stage matrix and workflow are an original synthesis, not a representation of any fund's private decision rules. Recheck current mandates, pricing and comparables when used for a live company.

- [Sequoia, The Arc Product-Market Fit Framework](https://sequoiacap.com/article/pmf-framework), published 2024-04-09: customer relationships with a problem differ, including emergent demand. The framework explicitly does not diagnose whether a company has PMF; neither should a label here.
- [Sequoia, The Arc PMF Terrifying Questions Framework](https://sequoiacap.com/article/pmf-framework-2), published 2025-02-12: connects company insight, opportunity and evidence of progress for pre-seed/seed founders. Use as a questioning approach, not a Seed or Series A funding checklist.
- [Bessemer, This Is Series A](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/this-is-series-a), published 2020-11-02, Shippo discussion: offers a firsthand example of stage metrics being business-specific and usage volume diverging from monetization. It is historical context, not a current numerical benchmark or representative sample.

No universal revenue, retention, valuation or fund-return threshold is derived from these readings. Do not use external commentary as evidence that the company under review has achieved a milestone.
