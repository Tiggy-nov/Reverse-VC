# Sources and limits

Reviewed 2026-09-27. The questions, evidence register and tests are an original synthesis. These readings motivate selected distinctions, not universal validation thresholds or claims about the company under review.

- [Sequoia, The Arc Product-Market Fit Framework](https://sequoiacap.com/article/pmf-framework), 2024-04-09: different customer relationships to a problem include urgent demand and emerging possibilities. This supports adapting discovery rather than insisting every valuable product solve an already-articulated urgent enterprise pain. It is not a diagnostic proof of PMF.
- [First Round Review, Vanta's Path to Product-Market Fit](https://review.firstround.com/vantas-path-to-product-market-fit/), founder interview/case history: describes learning from customer workflows, priorities and the commercial trigger behind compliance demand. This is one company's retrospective experience, not a representative study or required interview count. Do not import its numerical heuristics as pass marks.

Behavioral evidence, sample definitions and source attribution remain necessary even when a respected investor or founder endorses a method. A future-vision product may require prototypes and longer learning cycles; an immediately felt problem may still be poorly monetized or too limited for a particular fund.
