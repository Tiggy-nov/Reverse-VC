# VC GTM Stress Test

Can this company repeatedly reach the right customers, earn their trust and budget, turn purchase into delivered value, and retain or expand that value at sustainable acquisition and delivery cost?

Treat GTM as a core investment question. Test how distribution and delivered customer value reinforce each other; do not predetermine that one always outweighs the other. Strong acquisition with weak retention and strong product value with inaccessible buyers are different problems with different remedies.

## Company and fundraising lens

Default to AI-native B2B and enterprise businesses at Seed or Series A. Read [company-calibration.md](references/company-calibration.md) first, or reuse an established source-backed context: product/workflow, market and buyer, business model, actual maturity and proposed current raise. Apply only relevant tests and follow an explicit user scope override.

When the fundraising amount, timing or use of proceeds changes this review, read [raise-calibration.md](references/raise-calibration.md). Connect capital to the specific proof and operating milestones it buys; distinguish requested primary capital, secondary, secured funds and existing cash. If the amount is missing, continue the domain work and request it only when needed for the capital conclusion. A larger raise does not itself establish maturity, and a narrow test does not require a full financing audit.

State the applied business/stage lens and any material effect of the raise on the result. Keep current evidence, capital-plan feasibility and investor fit distinct; share definitions and source IDs across reviews.

## Establish the commercial model and scope

Use the requested question and available data first. Reconstruct the initial customer segment, job/trigger, user and buyer, budget, value proposition, price/contract, acquisition motion, route to production, and meaningful repeat use. Identify stage and actual maturity, geography, sales cycle and customer relationship to founders. Distinguish an AI-native product's GTM from merely using AI tools in sales/marketing; the skill can test either.

Inspect relevant room files: strategy and positioning, CRM stage/event history, channel spend and attribution, contracts/pilot terms, billing, product activation/cohorts, customer outcomes and churn, sales/implementation effort, hiring/ramp and partner terms. Cite file/version and exact page, row, event or cell. A complete room does not require a complete audit if the user asks one question.

Read [review-method.md](references/review-method.md) for shared evidence, stage, scope and authorization rules. Read only the relevant sections of [gtm-tests.md](references/gtm-tests.md). For funnels or economic claims, use [metrics-and-evidence.md](references/metrics-and-evidence.md). For requested stress scenarios, experiments or rehearsal, use [experiments-and-scenarios.md](references/experiments-and-scenarios.md).

## Follow the commercial chain

**Eligible customer and buying trigger → discovery/reach → understood value and credible proof → purchase decision → paid production or meaningful activation → retained use/value → expansion or referrals → acquisition payback and cash.**

Find the weakest evidenced link and test alternative explanations. Separate strategy fit, observed traction, repeatability and economic scalability. Treat attention, AI curiosity, friendly pilots, signed contracts, live deployments and durable revenue as distinct evidence. A useful early signal is not automatically repeatability; missing mature history is not automatically failure.

At Seed, assess a precise wedge, buyer learning, initial commitments and the next discriminating test. At Series A, inspect cohort maturity, conversion beyond friendly buyers, repeated outcomes, emerging channel/sales repeatability and measured economics. Adapt the route for B2B self-serve, developer, enterprise, marketplace, embedded/channel and relevant regulated workflows. Buyer size alone does not determine the motion. Apply consumer or growth-stage lenses only when the user explicitly requests that scope.

## Make the test decision-useful

Choose the requested domain or highest-consequence uncertainty. Each objection needs its source, causal mechanism, consequence, uncertainty and the evidence that would resolve it. Seek the strongest supported positive case too. Do not fill a fixed quota of objections, average unrelated dimensions into a spurious score, or declare product-market fit from one funnel ratio.

For AI businesses, explicitly consider the applicable issues: novelty versus repeat use, design-partner bias, pilot conversion, deployment/data/security friction, proof of accepted outcomes, human delivery effort, budget predictability, improved model substitutes and incumbent bundles. When AI is used for acquisition, test qualified outcomes and cost after human QA, failed attempts and reputation/deliverability effects; content or message volume alone is not productivity.

Use current primary sources for time-sensitive channel, platform or competitor claims. [research-notes.md](references/research-notes.md) records conceptual sources and limits, not a benchmark database. Founder case studies and investor opinion are hypotheses to apply selectively, not universal conversion or spending rules.

## Deliver the answer

For one requested test, return a concise source-backed finding, calculations, limits and next action. For a full GTM review, provide:

- A one-page commercial thesis: segment, trigger, buyer/budget, promise/proof, motion, channel, price, first value and reason to stay.
- A source-backed funnel/cohort and channel-economics assessment, with definitions and unmeasured stages visible.
- The strongest supported GTM advantage, the decisive failure mechanism, and dependencies on product, deployment, pricing or cash.
- A verdict scoped as promising discovery, evidence of repeatability in a named segment/channel, conditional scale case, supported breakdown, or insufficient evidence. Explain the stage and coverage; this is not an investment decision.
- A prioritized, costed experiment or corrective plan with proposed owner, eligible population, measure, criterion/basis, timing and the decision enabled. Mark work not run and costs unknown where necessary.

For requested material repair, create source-supported replacements and recheck affected claims. For recursive testing, follow the bounded review method; stronger wording cannot close missing business evidence. For live founder practice, ask one question and wait.

This skill is independent. In a suite, GTM owns the customer/channel/conversion/retention strategy; scalability owns capacity to deliver it, business model owns monetization/delivery economics, finance owns cash/model implementation, and competition owns durability. Reuse evidence and hand off material dependencies without requiring other skills or forcing a broader review. Do not contact customers, send campaigns, change ad budgets, deploy experiments or upload confidential material to another model without applicable authorization.
